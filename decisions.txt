query 1:
Just one MATCH on LineItem with a filter. No relationships: it's pure single-table aggregation, nothing to
traverse.

query 2:
Two steps: MATCH ... WITH p, min(sc.supplycost), then a second MATCH from the same p keeping only the
suppliers with sc2.supplycost = min_cost. Cypher has no correlated scalar subquery like SQL, so aggregating
in WITH and matching again is the correct way. After WITH only p and min_cost stay in scope.

query 3:
Customer -[:MAKES]-> Order -[:CONTAINS]-> LineItem, filtering segment, orderdate and shipdate.
The SQL joins disappear into the path; only the business filters stay in WHERE.

query 4:
Region <-[:IN_REGION]- Nation <-[:CUSTOMER_IN]- Customer -[:MAKES]-> Order -[:CONTAINS]-> LineItem
-[:SUPPLIED_BY]-> Supplier -[:SUPPLIER_IN]-> (the same Nation n).
"c_nationkey = s_nationkey" is not a property comparison: the path closes on the same Nation node.
Nation is a potential supernode because there are 25 nations and it's related to about 1,200 customers and 80 suppliers, so we directed the paths.
